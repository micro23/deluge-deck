# Sports heritage facts

Reviewed October 4, 2026. The UI contains factual identity only: team, city, establishment year, current venue and opening year, and championship count. Functional dashboard labels remain.

Establishment follows the franchise's commonly commemorated origin or first season; relocation does not restart the year or title total. The Cubs use their official 1876 anniversary convention. Venue dates refer to the current building's opening, rather than a sponsor rename or the first season a team played there. NFL counts are explicitly Super Bowl wins, excluding earlier NFL league championships. NBA counts include BAA titles. Canadiens totals include the 1916 pre-NHL Cup. These are a dated snapshot and should be reviewed before subsequent sports releases.

## Presentation checks

Original inline SVG equipment icons are bundled with the app. Baseball stitching, basketball seams, football laces, and hockey sticks/puck share a legible navy badge with separate download, upload, connections, and library marks. Team logos, venue artwork, lettering, palettes and material motifs supply the emotional identity without slogans. New factual text is checked at 7:1 contrast and icon strokes at 3:1; this is not a claim of whole-product WCAG AAA certification. Responsive theme verification covers all 24 clubs at 1456, 1200, 820, 390 and 320 pixels.
